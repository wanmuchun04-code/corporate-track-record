# Corporate Track Record — 企业行为档案

> 🇨🇳 **[中文版](README.zh-CN.md)**

**A source-backed, bilingual documentation repository of publicly
documented labor-conduct controversies — and of employers with
documented exemplary practice — including how downstream customers
responded, so job seekers and consumers can steer clear (or towards)
with evidence.**

Every entry is a *record*, not a verdict: it compiles what was publicly
reported, by whom, and when, with hyperlinks to the original articles,
regulatory notices, company filings and official websites. Nothing here
is legal advice. Inclusion ≠ finding of liability. If a fact changes,
the record is corrected — openly.

## Boards

- **⚫ Black list** — companies with verified labor-practice controversies
  (mass "persuaded resignations", coercive job transfers, retaliation
  against workers, contract breaches toward new hires, etc.).
- **⚪ Red list** — employers with a documented, source-backed exemplary
  record on worker treatment. Red entries follow the same rules: they
  also record documented controversies, so the picture stays complete.
- See **[METHODOLOGY](METHODOLOGY.md)** for inclusion criteria, evidence
  levels ([OFFICIAL]/[MEDIA]/[PARTY]/[MARKET]) and anti-misidentification
  rules. **Read it before relying on any entry.**

## How to use this repository

1. **Job seekers** — before accepting an offer, check whether the employer
   (or its parent) has an entry; read the *Implications for workers*
   section — on the black list **and** on the red list (culture fit is
   part of the data).
2. **Consumers & supply-chain watchers** — black-list cases show which
   downstream customers take labor compliance seriously and how (audit,
   statement, order pause, etc.).
3. **Researchers / journalists** — every claim carries a hyperlinked
   source (news article, official notice, company filing, official
   website); reuse with attribution (CC BY 4.0).

## Contributing & corrections

- New case → follow the template in **[CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md)**.
  Unsourced entries are rejected.
- Spot an error? Open an issue or PR; corrections are logged in the case
  file's *Corrections* section.
- Companies named here are welcome to respond — your reply, with a source,
  becomes part of the record.
