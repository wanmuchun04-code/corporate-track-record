## Summary

Xingyu hired ~440 graduates of the class of 2026 (mostly master's degree
holders, for R&D/technical/trainee roles) in July 2026 [2][7]. About one
month later, starting 2026-08-07/08, HR held mass one-on-one sessions
offering a "choice": resign voluntarily citing "personal reasons" for a
half-month salary, or be transferred to production-line operator roles
(assembly, lamp-plugging, screw fastening) with pay re-rated to general
worker levels [2][4][7]. ~107 of the 440 were ultimately released [3][7].

The Changzhou Human Resources and Social Security Bureau (人社局) found
the consultation process "crude, lacking adequate and effective
communication, causing adverse effects" ([OFFICIAL]) [2][3]. The company
published an apology letter on 2026-08-27 with compensation and
job-search support [2][7]. On 2026-09-01 Volkswagen Group China confirmed
it had launched a special investigation into the supplier after receiving
complaints ([OFFICIAL]) [3][8]. Mercedes-Benz's Business & People
Protection Office replied to complainants that the described conduct
"does not conform to Mercedes-Benz's corporate principles" and forwarded
it for expert review, as reported by Jiemian, ZAKER Finance and Sanyan
Tech [MEDIA, not publicly confirmed by Mercedes] [9][11][12].

## Key facts

- **Hiring vs. release:** ~440 graduates onboarded July 2026; ~107
  released; a graduate-run group reportedly shrank from ~400+ to ~120
  members [MEDIA] [4][7].
- **The "choice":** resign as "personal reasons" + 0.5 month salary, or
  forced re-assignment to line-operator roles with pay re-rated to
  general-worker standard [MEDIA] [2][4].
- **Alleged coercion:** HR reportedly pressured via background-check
  implications ("a Changzhou HR group of 400+ people; background checks
  only stay clean if you sign") — reported from leaked recordings;
  recordings' authenticity not independently established [PARTY/MEDIA] [4][7].
- **Regulatory finding [OFFICIAL]:** per the Changzhou HRSSB notice of
  2026-08-25 — 107 labour contracts terminated; process "simple and
  crude, lacking adequate effective communication"; company apologised;
  HR director suspended; **no** improper receipt of employment/talent
  subsidies found; by that date 22 of 107 had found new jobs, 14 were in
  interviews [2][3][7].
- **Financial context [OFFICIAL]:** H1 2026 revenue RMB 6.884bn (+1.87%
  y/y), attributable net profit RMB 669m (−5.26% y/y); ~RMB 2.7bn cash at
  2026-06-30; interim dividend of RMB 2.00 per 10 shares (~RMB 56.64m)
  approved [2][8]. Q2 alone: revenue −5.7%, net profit −18.3% y/y [7].
- **Staffing trend [OFFICIAL]:** headcount 10,426 (end-2024) → 7,532
  (end-2025), net −2,894 (−27.76%); production staff 7,911 → 4,729;
  outsourced-labour hours rose from 8.638m (2024) to 10.874m (2025,
  +25.9%) and 2.384m (2022) → 10.874m (2025, ~3.6x over 3 years);
  outsourcing pay RMB 302m in 2025 [4][7][15].
- **Capital-markets context:** second application for H-share listing on
  HKEX filed 2026-07-29 (first had lapsed) [2][7].

## Company's own response

- **2026-08-27 apology letter [OFFICIAL]:** management admitted "errors in
  decision-making, omissions in management; rigid communication and crude
  methods in execution, failing to consider the students' interests" [2][7].
- **Remediation offered:** 3-month job-search living subsidy (~RMB 5,000/
  month per graduate, paid out — recipients confirmed RMB 15,000 by Aug 27);
  free dormitory during the period; company-assisted job referrals and
  participation in HRSSB-organised job fairs; if not employed by end of
  November, an additional 6 months' salary [2][7].
- **People measures:** HR director suspended (per HRSSB notice) [2][3].
- **2026-09-02 briefing [OFFICIAL]:** chairman apologised again; stated
  remediation under government guidance; share buyback "will be
  implemented when appropriate" [6].

## Implications for workers

1. **Fresh-graduate status is the real asset at risk.** Under current
  rules, once social insurance is paid, a released graduate may lose the
  "fresh graduate" (应届生) status that gates many state/civil-service
  and SOE campus-recruitment channels — monetary compensation cannot
  restore it. Reported as the core grievance [4][7].
2. **Read the rotation clause.** Where a "production rotation" is
  promised, have the duration in writing; Xingyu's was reported extended
  from 1 to 3 months unilaterally [2][7].
3. **Don't sign "personal reasons" under pressure.** Signing converts a
  company-initiated termination into a resignation and typically waives
  severance; HRSSB criticised exactly this process here [2][4].
4. **Check the annual report headcount line.** At listed employers,
  staff-count and outsourcing trends are disclosed — a large decline with
  surging outsourced hours (at Xingyu: −27.8% headcount in 2025 while
  outsourced hours tripled) is a cheap pre-employment signal [4][7][15].
5. **Evidence + channels.** Recordings/notices/contracts were the basis of
  media verification here; recourse channels include labour inspection
  (劳动监察) and labour arbitration (劳动仲裁) [4][7].
